About

I'm a senior in electrical engineering at Mississippi State University, with minors in mathematics and artificial intelligence, and I'm planning to continue on to a master's degree. Most of my work so far has been in embedded systems, sensor integration, and instrumentation.

I interned in instrumentation and controls design at Entergy Nuclear, did cleanroom research on MEMS gyroscopes at Georgia Tech, and work with microcontrollers and I²C sensors as a research assistant at MSU's Center for Advanced Vehicle Systems. I've also worked as a maintenance technician since 2021.

On campus I work with Supplemental Instruction, where I started as an SI leader running Microprocessors sessions and was promoted to SI mentor; I now oversee other engineering SI leaders as they lead their sessions. I also mentor in the ECE Foundations lab and serve as a Bagley College of Engineering Ambassador for the ECE department. I'm a member of the Shackouls Honors College, Tau Beta Pi, and the Society of Women Engineers.
